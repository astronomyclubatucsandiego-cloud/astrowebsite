**Our annual Anza Borrego trip!**

Join Astronomy Club members and friends on a weekend trip to this state park! Activities will include hiking through a stunning desert landscape, camping under the stars, card games, learning how to build a campfire, and enjoying the beauty of the cosmos through the lens of telescopes.

Please contact the Astronomy Club with any questions in our Discord, Instagram, or at astronomyclubatucsandiego@gmail.com
