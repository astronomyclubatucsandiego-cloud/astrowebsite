In collaboration with the San Diego Astronomy Association, the Astronomy Club organized an overnight stargazing trip at the Tierra Del Sol Darksite. Away from the bustling city's light pollution, our members were able to peer through small and big telescopes to see various objects in the night sky, as well as observe a multitude of stars with only the naked eye!
