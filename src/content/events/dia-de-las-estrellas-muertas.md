Día de las Estrellas Muertas 🧡💀 Hosted in collaboration between Astronomy Club at UC San Diego and Latinx Advancing in Math and Physics (LAMP) at UC San Diego.

Join us for Día de las Estrellas Muertas, a Day of the Dead celebration that brings together culture, community, and the night sky. We’ll have hot chocolate, conchas, and pan de muerto, along with rounds of space-themed lotería before heading out to observe the stars. This is a space to connect, unwind, and celebrate Latinx presence in STEM while enjoying good food and good company. Everyone is welcome!
